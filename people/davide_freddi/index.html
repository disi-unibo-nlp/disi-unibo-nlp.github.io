Group leadership

Gianluca Moro

Head of the Group

Biography

Gianluca Moro, Ph.D., is an associate professor in natural language processing, text mining and big data analytics, directing the NLP research group at Bologna’s Cesena campus. He co-organized workshops at major conferences like VLDB, AAMAS, ECIR etc. and has published over 110 papers in leading AI journals and conferences, including AAAI, EMNLP, IJCAI, ECAI, ACL, ICLR, etc., serving also on conference program committees and journal editorial boards. Prof. Moro has led multiple national and international AI projects in partnership with public and private research institutions.
